Omega Strikers

Footbrawler
2023

The first title for a new studio, Omega Strikers is a multiplayer top-down footbrawler that blends sports games like Windjammers with MOBAs like League of Legends.

As the sole technical designer at the studio, I worked on a lot of features, including:

  • Character design support and implementation
  • Game mode design and implementation
  • Gamepad and touch control design and implementation (layout, auto-assist, QoL features)
  • Tutorial and Practice mode design and implementation

Notable Mentions:
- Nominated for Online Game of the Year at the DICE Awards 2023
